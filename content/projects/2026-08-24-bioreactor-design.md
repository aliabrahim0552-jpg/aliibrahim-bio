**3D CAD Design of a 15L Laboratory Bioreactor**



This 3D engineering model was developed using \*\*SolidWorks\*\* for bioengineering and bioprocess applications.



**Key Design Features:**

**Vessel & Thermal Jacket:** Designed for standard batch capacity and precise temperature control.

**Agitation System (Agitator Shaft & Impellers):** Ensures efficient mixing and biomass homogenization.

**Internal Baffles:** Prevents vortex formation and optimizes fluid dynamics.

**Ports & Gas Sparger:** Integrated ports for sensor monitoring, nutrient feed, and gas delivery.
